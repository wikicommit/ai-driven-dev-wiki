## Summary

A German-language codecentric blog post by Goetz Markgraf (April 2025) examining the hype around "vibe coding", the term Andrej Karpathy coined in February 2025 for building software with AI tools while ignoring the code. It distinguishes vibe coding from ordinary AI-assisted development, lists the strengths and weaknesses of generative AI in software development as observed in client projects, and points out that Karpathy himself framed it as suitable for throwaway weekend projects. The author sees professional uses in rapid prototyping, idea generation and well-specified individual modules, but would never put a vibe-coded application into production.
