## Summary

The fourth post in a codecentric series on AI-supported development presents the company's internal Loop Model of AI Engineering: four loops — Coding, Validation, Learning and Business — in which the three technical loops are nested and the Business Loop is connected to them through Enterprise Context Management. The post argues that when AI makes code cheap the bottleneck shifts to verification and to deciding what to build, so optimising the coding loop alone optimises locally and loses globally, and that the bridge from business needs into the coding loop is the hardest and least addressed part of the system.
