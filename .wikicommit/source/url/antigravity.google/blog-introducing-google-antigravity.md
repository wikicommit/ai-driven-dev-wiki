## Summary

The Antigravity Team's launch post of November 18, 2025 introduces Google Antigravity, an agentic development platform that pairs an AI-powered Editor view with an agent-first Manager surface for spawning and observing multiple agents asynchronously across workspaces. It organises the product around four tenets — trust (agents report through Artifacts such as task lists, implementation plans, walkthroughs, screenshots and browser recordings), autonomy, feedback (comments on Artifacts folded into the agent's run without stopping it) and self-improvement (a knowledge base agents read from and add to). It launched in public preview at no charge for individuals on macOS, Linux and Windows, with Gemini 3, Claude Sonnet 4.5 and GPT-OSS available to the agent.
