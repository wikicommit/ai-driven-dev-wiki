## Summary

The Coalition for Secure AI's Workstream 4 report on Model Context Protocol security, approved by its Project Governing Board on 8 January 2026 and still marked Status: Draft. It organises nearly forty threats into twelve categories across three specificity tiers, maps eleven control families against them, analyses three deployment patterns, and cites the Asana, WordPress AI Engine and Supabase incidents as evidence the threats are active rather than theoretical. Its argument for needing a distinct threat model is that existing AI risk frameworks assume components behave predictably, whereas MCP places a natural-language-driven LLM at the centre of security-critical decisions.
