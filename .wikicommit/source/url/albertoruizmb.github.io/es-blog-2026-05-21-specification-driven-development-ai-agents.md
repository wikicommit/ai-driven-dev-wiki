## Summary

A May 2026 Spanish-language blog post by Alberto Ruiz arguing that AI coding agents are bringing specifications back to the centre of development through Specification-Driven Development (SDD). It explains SDD as defining what to build, why, and under which constraints and validation criteria before implementation, so that the specification becomes a persistent, reviewable source of truth shared by people and agents rather than intent living in ephemeral prompts. It surveys the still-open tool ecosystem (Kiro, Spec Kit, Tessl, OpenSpec) and recommends adopting SDD with judgement and human oversight rather than as a promise of total automation.
