## Summary

Anthropic's 2026 Agentic Coding Trends Report, forecasting eight trends for agentic coding grouped as foundation, capability and impact trends. Its recurring theme is what it calls the collaboration paradox — developers using AI in roughly 60% of their work while reporting they can "fully delegate" only 0-20% of tasks — which it resolves by arguing the practice is collaborative rather than a handover. Its evidence base is internal research plus named customer case studies, all reported by the customers themselves.
