## Summary

A Toss Payments engineer argues that the wide variation in what engineers get out of the same model is a gap in know-how about controlling the tool rather than in coding ability, and that leaving that gap to individual aptitude is a loss the organization bears. The proposal is to distribute a team's working methods through a coding agent's plugin and marketplace mechanism: knowledge that reads as a guideline when a person reads it and as a system prompt when a model does, layered into company-wide, team-domain and repository scopes, and argued to be more predictable than a retrieval-augmented pipeline. The author repeatedly frames the whole argument as a direction and a hypothesis rather than a demonstrated result.
