## Summary

A Toss DevRel manager describes AI Surf Day, a programme in which Toss teams spent every Friday from April to June experimenting with AI and applying it to their own work, presented as a cultural device for making Toss a company that works on the basis of AI. The post sets out its fixed programmes — employee-run AI Surf Clubs (about 200 formed at launch), AI Surf Weekly sessions for sharing internal use cases, and 142 AI Surf Evangelists nominated by colleagues to spread AI practice within their teams — and a one-off collaboration day with OpenAI with Codex and ChatGPT Agent sessions and a mini-hackathon. The author concludes that what the programme left behind was protected time and a culture of sharing rather than any particular programme.
