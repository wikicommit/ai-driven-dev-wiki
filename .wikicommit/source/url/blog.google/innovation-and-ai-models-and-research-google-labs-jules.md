## Summary
A Google Labs announcement post marking Jules, Google's asynchronous coding agent, entering public beta worldwide with no waitlist on 20 May 2025. It describes Jules as an agent rather than a completion assistant: it clones a repository into a secure Google Cloud VM, reasons over the project's full context, and performs tasks such as writing tests, building features, fixing bugs, bumping dependency versions and producing audio changelogs, presenting its plan, reasoning and a diff on completion. The post lists the properties Google claims for it - working on real codebases rather than a sandbox, parallel execution in the cloud VM, a visible plan before changes, GitHub integration, user-steerable plans, and audio summaries - and states that it runs on Gemini 2.5 Pro and does not train on private code.
