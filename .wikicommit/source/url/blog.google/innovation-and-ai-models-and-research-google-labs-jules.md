## Summary

Google Labs post of 20 May 2025 by Kathy Korevec announcing that Jules, its asynchronous coding agent, was entering public beta with no waitlist. It sets out Jules' cloud-VM architecture, its task list, its privacy-by-default claim, its Gemini 2.5 Pro model, and six capabilities including parallel execution, a visible plan, GitHub integration, user steerability and audio changelogs. Its framing claim — that "agentic development is shifting from prototype to product" — is recorded as Google's own assertion, as are the capability claims; the post contains no independent evaluation.
