## Summary

A French-language blog post (February 2026) surveying how development teams industrialised AI coding assistants in 2025, drawing on testimony from engineers at several tech companies. It argues that vibe coding has given way to "Context Driven Engineering" — supplying agents with the full context of intent and constraints — built around a spec/plan/act workflow, and covers the ecosystem of AI rules (agents.md files, skills, agents, MCP servers), internal marketplaces for sharing them, CI/CD integration and evaluation of prompt changes, per-developer costs, intellectual-property concerns, and the human effects on hiring, junior training, onboarding and the developer's role.
