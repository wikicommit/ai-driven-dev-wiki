## Summary

A short paper presented at SAEI 2025 (54 JAIIO) that describes the design and implementation of a roughly 25-hour course on AI-assisted software development for professionals without formal software-engineering training, first taught as an elective in a business master's programme at Universidad Torcuato Di Tella in March 2024. The course is organized around SDLC phases in five modules (version control, Python packaging, debugging, testing/TDD, and CI/CD and release) with GitHub Copilot in Visual Studio Code used throughout, and the paper reports high student satisfaction alongside friction with tool installation, and discusses the risks of vibe coding for learners.
