## Summary

Rainer Gibbert's April 2026 German-language post on produktbezogen.de looks at spec-driven development from the product-management side, arguing that writing a structured spec before handing work to an AI coding agent is essentially product discovery under a new name. It describes how AI-accelerated implementation leaves product and UX struggling to keep up with requirements work, and urges product managers and UX to lead the discover phase of the SDD workflow, make discovery outputs spec-compatible, and establish joint spec reviews with engineering to avoid a "SpecFall" of specs written in isolation.
