## Summary

A 2023 Chinese-language whitepaper from the Unit Mesh open-source initiative on how generative AI reshapes software development, covering its effect on individual and team productivity, on software architecture, and on scenarios such as requirements engineering, end-to-end coding assistance, quality improvement and model fine-tuning with high-quality corpora.
