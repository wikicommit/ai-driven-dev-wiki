## Summary

A Chinese-language blog post summarizing the arXiv survey "A Survey of Vibe Coding with Large Language Models", walking through the paper's definition of vibe coding as a three-party relationship between the human developer, the software project and the coding agent, and its taxonomy of five development models. It relays the survey's account of why experienced developers can be slower with advanced AI tools — context engineering, feedback loops and infrastructure rather than model capability alone — and its list of the challenges the practice raises for reliability, oversight, human adaptation and education. The author adds a framing of their own: that the common failure is treating a coding agent as a tool rather than as an employee to be managed, which produces either blind acceptance or excessive distrust.
