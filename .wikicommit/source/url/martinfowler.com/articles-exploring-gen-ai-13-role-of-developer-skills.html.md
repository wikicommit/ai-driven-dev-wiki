## Summary

Birgitta Böckeler's memo of 25 March 2025, published on martinfowler.com as part of the "Exploring Gen AI" series, cataloguing concrete cases where she had to steer agentic coding assistants while working almost exclusively on existing codebases. It organises the missteps by impact radius — time to commit, team flow within an iteration, and long-term maintainability — noting that the wider the radius the longer the feedback loop, and closes with safeguards for individual coders and for teams and organisations.
