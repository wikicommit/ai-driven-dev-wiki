## Summary

Cursor research post of 6 November 2025 by Stefan Heule, Emily Jia and Naman Jain, reporting that giving its coding agent semantic search alongside grep raised answer accuracy by 12.5% on average across the models tested, with smaller effects in an online A/B test on real traffic. It also describes how Cursor's own embedding model is trained: agent session traces are ranked by an LLM for what should have been retrieved earlier, and the model is trained to align its similarity scores with those rankings. Every figure is the vendor's own, measured on its own benchmark and user base, which the generated pages state explicitly.
