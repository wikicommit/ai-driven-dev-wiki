## Summary

A glossary entry from Addy Osmani's agentic engineering glossary defining skill atrophy as the gradual loss of an engineering skill through disuse when AI agents take over the work that used to exercise it, drawing analogies to autopilot-dependent pilots and surgeons who operate infrequently. It argues that agentic engineering depends on strong fundamentals because review, specification writing and architectural judgement all require deep understanding of code, and states the paradox that better AI makes deep code understanding more important rather than less. It lists six counter-practices: intentional hand-written practice, deep review, debugging first without AI, explaining code to colleagues as a comprehension test, balanced delegation of routine work, and using AI as a learning accelerator rather than a replacement.
