## Summary

The third part of a French-language blog series on organizing development teams for AI, which revisits the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) as a governance framework for agentic development. It contrasts the traditional six-stage SDLC with a proposed "augmented SDLC" of five continuous moments (Discover, Design, Build, Validate & Deploy, Learn & Evolve) in which the organization's shared body of knowledge, rather than code, becomes the main asset, and it draws on strategic Domain-Driven Design, Team Topologies' cognitive-load framing and the idea of an AI harness. It argues that decisions that define a product's identity should not be delegated to AI.
