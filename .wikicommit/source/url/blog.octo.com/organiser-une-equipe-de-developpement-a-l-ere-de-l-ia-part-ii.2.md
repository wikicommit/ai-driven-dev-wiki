## Summary

The second half of part II of a French-language blog series on organizing development teams for AI, arguing that value shifts from code to an explicit body of business knowledge. Domain-Driven Design structures that knowledge, Behavior-Driven Development (via Example Mapping workshops and Gherkin scenarios) makes its behaviours explicit, and Spec-Driven Development gathers both into a living, versioned specification that guides teams and agents, with the degree of agent autonomy varying by subdomain. It closes with the pitfalls of the DDD/BDD/SDD trio, such as over- and under-specification, duplicated knowledge and frozen specifications.
