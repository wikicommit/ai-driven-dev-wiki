## Summary

A German-language guide (version 1.0) from an OSSBIG working group on the technical use of generative AI in organizations, with a focus on AI-assisted software development, written for IT managers, operations staff, decision-makers and developers. It covers the benefits of AI across the development process, how LLMs work, how their output is evaluated and where they are weak, the trade-offs of closed-source versus open-source/open-weights models and of on-premise versus cloud operation, prompt engineering, LLM integration, MLOps and security, and closes with the group's own experiments: an open-source IDE coding assistant, fine-tuning StarCoder on two codebases, a CUDA port of llama2.c, and self-hosted and Azure chatbots.
