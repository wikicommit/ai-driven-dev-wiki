## Summary

An IEEE Spectrum news article of 8 April 2025 by Matthew S. Smith, reporting early practitioner experience with vibe coding about two months after the term was named. It is built on three interviews — a mechanical engineer who built internal tools, a data engineering lead who uses it for rapid proofs of concept, and a software engineer of 25 years who disputes that either of them was really vibe coding — and records that all three agree it works best as an accelerator for gaining skills in unfamiliar languages and stacks.
