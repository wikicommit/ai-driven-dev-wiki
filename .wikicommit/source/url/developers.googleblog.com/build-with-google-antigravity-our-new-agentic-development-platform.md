## Summary

Google's announcement of Antigravity, which it describes as an agentic development platform rather than an editor, combining a familiar AI-powered coding experience with an agent-first Manager surface where agents are spawned, orchestrated and observed working asynchronously across workspaces. Agents plan, execute and verify tasks across the editor, terminal and browser, and report through Artifacts — task lists, implementation plans, screenshots and browser recordings — which a developer reviews and comments on instead of reading raw tool-call logs. At announcement it was in public preview, free for individuals, cross-platform, with Gemini 3 Pro plus support for Claude Sonnet 4.5 and GPT-OSS.
