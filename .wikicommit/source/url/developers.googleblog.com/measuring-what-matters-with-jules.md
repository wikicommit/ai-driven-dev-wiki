## Summary

A June 2026 Google Developers Blog post by Google Labs researchers arguing that proactive coding agents, which pursue goals rather than well-defined tasks, need evaluation of their insight policy rather than task completion. It describes building a preliminary ground truth by clustering a team's historical bug fixes by temporal proximity and semantic similarity (705 bugs, 1,178 CLs from internal Google codebases), and reports an average top score of 4.5 out of 5 with one exploration round and Hit@5 rising from 33% to 57% when the exploration budget grew from two rounds to three.
