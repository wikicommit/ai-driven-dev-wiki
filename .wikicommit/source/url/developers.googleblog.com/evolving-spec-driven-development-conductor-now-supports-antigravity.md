## Summary

Google announces that Conductor, its spec-driven development tool for the terminal, is evolving from a Gemini CLI extension into the Conductor Plugin, a package that can bundle skills, rules, MCP servers and hooks. As a plugin it works conversationally rather than through strict command sequences, while still keeping spec.md and plan.md as persistent Markdown artifacts, and it becomes usable from tools other than Gemini CLI, including Antigravity CLI. The post claims backwards compatibility with existing Conductor commands, plans and specs, and a higher success rate on the most complex subset of TerminalBench tasks than working without spec-driven development.
