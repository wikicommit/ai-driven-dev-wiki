## Summary

Anthropic's economic research report of 16 June 2026 analysing roughly 400,000 interactive Claude Code sessions from about 235,000 people between October 2025 and April 2026. Its two central findings are recorded as a Report page: a stable division of labor in which people make about 70% of planning decisions and about 20% of execution decisions, and returns to task-specific domain expertise that persist while returns to a coding background largely do not — every one of the ten largest occupation groups lands within seven percentage points of software engineers on code-producing sessions. The Claude Code page gained an observed-usage section from the same data, and the agentic coding page the measured form of the division of labor.
