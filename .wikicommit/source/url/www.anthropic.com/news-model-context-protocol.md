## Summary

Anthropic's announcement of 25 November 2024 open-sourcing the Model Context Protocol, framing the problem as models being trapped behind information silos and legacy systems with a custom implementation needed per data source. It names the three launch components, the pre-built servers for Google Drive, Slack, GitHub, Git, Postgres and Puppeteer, the early adopters Block and Apollo alongside Zed, Replit, Codeium and Sourcegraph, and credits the protocol's creation at Anthropic to David Soria Parra and Justin Spahr-Summers.
