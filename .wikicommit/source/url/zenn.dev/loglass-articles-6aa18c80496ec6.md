## Summary

A Loglass tech blog post recommending that, rather than simply asking an AI to "review" its output, users ask it to perform adversarial verification: a reviewer with a fresh context that assumes the work has a problem, tries to refute it, and returns verdicts with grounds. It describes how the author sees the pattern built into Claude Code, gives four requirements and a prompt template for other tools, sets out its limits, and concludes that a human must decide whether to accept, weaken or reject each finding.
