## Summary

An analytical survey by Singh, Ehtesham, Kumar, Talaei Khoei and Vasilakos of Agentic RAG — retrieval-augmented generation with autonomous agents embedded in the pipeline, drawing on reflection, planning, tool use and multi-agent collaboration to manage retrieval dynamically. Its case is that traditional RAG is constrained by static workflows and lacks the adaptability needed for multi-step reasoning. It does not claim the term, attributing the convergence that gave rise to it to earlier work; what it presents as its own is a taxonomy of architectures by agent cardinality, control structure, autonomy and knowledge representation. Its application domains are outside this wiki's scope; the structural claim about agent-mediated retrieval is what carries over.
