## Summary

A preprint arguing that while vibe coding is valuable for ideation and prototyping, enterprise-grade software requires specification governance to turn probabilistic AI generation into deterministic, auditable engineering. It consolidates the verified empirical record into four recurring failure modes of ungoverned conversational generation — a productivity–reliability paradox, architectural erosion, security exposure and technical-debt accumulation — and introduces the Specification Governance Reference Model (SGRM), a tool-independent framework that defines specifications as four-component contracts, encloses a stochastic generator inside a deterministic validation boundary, and formalizes three tiers of specification rigor. It evaluates SGRM analytically against ISO/IEC 25010 and states its thesis as conditional on enterprise-grade quality-attribute demands being present.
