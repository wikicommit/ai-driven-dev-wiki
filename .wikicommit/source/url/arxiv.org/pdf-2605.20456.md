## Summary

This paper argues that the central problem in agentic AI development is no longer prompt engineering but engineering process control, and synthesizes evidence from productivity trials, GitHub-scale adoption studies, repository-configuration research, issue-resolution benchmarks, and hardware verification benchmarks to support that claim. It proposes Agentic Agile-V, a process framework combining the Agile-V lifecycle as a macro backbone with a task-level SCOPE-V loop (Specify, Constrain, Orchestrate, Prove, Evolve, Verify), and adds a conversation-to-contract gate, a minimum input artifact model, risk-adaptive acceptance gates, and an evidence-bundle acceptance model for agent-generated artifacts.
