## Summary

A June 2026 paper showing that context compaction silently deletes the in-context governance constraints an agent had been reliably obeying, raising tool-call violation rates from 0% to 30% across seven model families and 1,323 episodes. It produced the most pages of any source in this run: the paper itself, the named phenomenon (governance decay), its benchmark (ConstraintRot), and substantial additions to compaction — where it reframes the operation as "a first-class agent-governance surface" rather than only an economic decision — and to indirect prompt injection, for the Compaction-Eviction Attack that induces the failure deliberately by deleting rather than inserting.
