## Summary

A July 2026 preprint by researchers at Wavestone AI Lab and Inclusive Brains that dissects the source code of eleven production coding-agent harnesses (Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Mistral Vibe, OpenHands, Aider, Mini-SWE-Agent, Hermes, Pi, OpenCode and OpenClaw) plus the Omnigent meta-harness along seven canonical subsystems. It reports 13 cross-cutting observations and 29 recurring design patterns, including the absence of general-purpose agentic frameworks and of embedding-based code retrieval in every system, a quarter-long longitudinal diff showing convergence turning into imitation, and a thesis that the harness turned from tool into platform in the first half of 2026. It closes with 18 design recommendations and a 90-line minimum-viable-harness scaffold.
