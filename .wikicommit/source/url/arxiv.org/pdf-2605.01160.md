## Summary

A single-author preprint arguing that AI coding assistants produce individual-level productivity gains that coexist systematically with system-level reliability degradation — a phenomenon it names the Productivity-Reliability Paradox and grounds in a multivocal review of 67 sources published between January 2022 and April 2026. It proposes two further frameworks of its own, the AI-Augmented Methodology Taxonomy and the Specification Governance Model derived from Transaction Cost Economics, and evaluates Spec Kit and the TDAD pipeline as instantiations, supported by an explicitly illustrative four-month pilot across three teams. Its thesis is that specification discipline, not model capability, is the binding constraint on AI-assisted software dependability.
