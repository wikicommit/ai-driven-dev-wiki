## Summary

This FSE '26 paper reports a grounded-theory study of 20 vibe coding videos, seven live-streamed sessions and 13 opinion videos, on how practitioners define and engage in vibe coding. It finds a spectrum from coders who never inspect code to those who read and adapt it, and shows that all must contend with the stochastic nature of generation, so that debugging and refinement feel like rolling the dice. Coders' mental models, shaped by expertise and reliance on AI, influence their prompting, evaluation and trust, with implications for tool design and education.
