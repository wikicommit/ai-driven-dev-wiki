## Summary

An MSR '26 empirical study testing industry claims that code review agents can handle 80% of pull requests without human involvement. Analysing 3,109 pull requests from the AIDev dataset, it finds agent-only reviewed PRs merge at 45.20% against 68.37% for human-only ones, with abandonment of 34.88% against 21.60%, and traces the gap to feedback quality: 60.2% of closed agent-only PRs carry comment sets in which under a third of comments are actionable, and 12 of 13 agents average below 60% on that measure. Its recommendation is that review agents augment rather than replace human reviewers.
