## Summary

"Agentic Design Patterns: A System-Theoretic Framework" (Dao, Le, Lam, Le, Pham, O'Sullivan, Nguyen; NeurIPS 2025 workshop) proposes a system-theoretic architecture that deconstructs an agentic AI system into five core functional subsystems, derives a catalogue of 12 reusable agentic design patterns from that architecture, and demonstrates the framework's utility through a qualitative case study diagnosing and prescribing improvements to the ReAct framework.
