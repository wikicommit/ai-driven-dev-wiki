## Summary

This technical report formalises Spec-Driven Agentic Development (SDAD), which pairs disciplined up-front specification with agentic synthesis in four steps: intent capture, machine-readable specification, agentic synthesis, and independent multi-agent verification under human sign-off. It reads the history of software methodology as a pendulum between Waterfall and Agile, introduces AI-code as a fourth production paradigm, and recasts the engineer as a Spec Architect. It proposes metrics and cost models such as the Ambiguity Tax, Spec Fidelity, and the Synthesis Efficiency Ratio, along with hybrid estimation and a staged migration blueprint, arguing that agentic speed relocates engineering discipline upstream rather than eliminating it.
