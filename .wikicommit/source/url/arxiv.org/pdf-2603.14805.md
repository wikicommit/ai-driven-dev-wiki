## Summary

A preprint by Gal Bakal of Yahoo Inc. arguing that "the bottleneck to effective agentic software development is not model capability but knowledge architecture", and proposing the Atomic Knowledge Unit — an AI Skill specialized for institutional knowledge delivery — as the primitive that fixes it. Its contribution to this wiki is a concrete account of what a governance-aware skill would add to the open standard: permission boundaries and compliance requirements, continuation paths linking skills into a navigable graph, deterministic pre- and post-execution validators, and an enterprise metadata vocabulary covering team ownership, service tier, SLA, environment, on-call rotation and cost centre. Its evidence is one enterprise deployment of 87 skills at Yahoo surveyed across 67 engineers, which the author frames explicitly as an existence proof supporting a structural rather than a magnitude claim, and whose limits he reports in detail.
