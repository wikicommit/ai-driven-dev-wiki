## Summary

A practitioner's guide to spec-driven development (SDD) for AI coding assistants, presenting three levels of specification rigor (spec-first, spec-anchored, spec-as-source), a four-phase specify/plan/implement/validate workflow, a survey of tools from BDD/TDD frameworks to AI-assisted toolkits like GitHub Spec Kit, Amazon Kiro, and Tessl, three case studies (API-first microservices, BDD for enterprise features, model-based embedded development), and a decision framework for when SDD is worth its overhead.
