## Summary

A large multi-institution survey unifying post-training, retrieval, memory, and skill systems under a single notion of adaptation — improving an agent, its tools, or their interaction after pretraining. Its page records the four-paradigm framework (A1 tool-execution-signaled and A2 agent-output-signaled on the agent side; T1 agent-agnostic and T2 agent-supervised on the tool side), which is useful here because it names the axis practitioner vocabulary blurs: skills and memory are tool adaptation, and the tool side is where a coding-agent operator's levers actually are.
