## Summary

A position paper proposing GROUNDING.md, a community-governed, field-scoped document holding a domain's non-negotiable validity rules (Hard Constraints) and agreed defaults (Convention Parameters), positioned above the plan, project, and technique layers so its constraints override them. The concept got its own DefinedTerm page and the context files page gained the layering argument.
