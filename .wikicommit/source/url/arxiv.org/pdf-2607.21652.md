## Summary

A multivocal literature review of vibe coding that synthesizes 47 sources (28 peer-reviewed and 19 grey) published between 2022 and October 2025 under a single documented protocol, across eight research questions. It finds vibe coding consistently described as an iterative generation–evaluation–revision loop rather than a one-shot activity, with developer work shifting from writing code toward specification, supervision and validation; short-term productivity and time-to-prototype gains are reported in 21 of 47 sources, while evidence on maintainability, long-term quality and safeguard effectiveness remains limited. It frames vibe coding as an iterative control system, labels each finding with an evidence-strength rating, and reports the evidence as strongest for prototyping and user-interface work and weakest for production, data-intensive and safety-critical use.
