## Summary

A qualitative analysis of 57 curated YouTube videos published between late 2024 and 2025, capturing industrial practitioners' accounts of LLM coding tools — a vantage point the authors argue prior research has left underexplored. Its page records the three-approach spectrum practitioners themselves describe (vibe coding, AI-assisted coding, agentic coding, differing in AI autonomy and human involvement), the finding that bottlenecks shift toward review, testing, security assessment, and system-level reasoning rather than disappearing, and the skill-erosion concern the paper carries into an argument for curricular change.

No entities were excluded. The page states clearly that YouTube commentary captures what practitioners say rather than what they do; the four authors are named only as authors, so no Person pages were created.
