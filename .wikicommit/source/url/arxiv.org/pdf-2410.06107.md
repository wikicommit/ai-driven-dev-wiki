## Summary

A vision paper proposing Software Engineering 3.0 (SE 3.0), an AI-native approach centred on intent-centric, conversation-oriented development between human developers and AI teammates. It analyses the limitations of today's AI-assisted SE 2.0, outlines an SE 3.0 technology stack (Teammate.next, IDE.next, Compiler.next, Runtime.next and FM.next), and sets out a roadmap of open challenges.
