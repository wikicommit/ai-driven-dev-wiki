## Summary

The ICLR 2026 paper introducing ACE, which treats an agent's context as an evolving playbook updated by incremental deltas rather than rewritten whole. Its two named failure modes are the more transferable contribution and drove a new page: context collapse — a context of 18,282 tokens at 66.7 accuracy compressing to 122 tokens at 57.1 in a single adaptation step, below the 63.7 no-adaptation baseline — and brevity bias. The agentic memory page gained the delta-update discipline as an answer to destroying a note by maintaining it, and the context rot page gained the distinction between degraded recall over present material and material removed by a rewrite.

No entities were excluded. The paper's thirteen authors are named only as authors, so no Person pages were created; ACE itself is a research framework rather than a released tool, so it is recorded on the paper's page rather than as a SoftwareApplication.
