## Summary
A survey (DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3675554) of the security risks specific to agentic AI systems — LLM-based agents with planning, tool use, memory and autonomy. It proposes a taxonomy of threats (prompt injection and jailbreaks, autonomous cyber-exploitation and tool abuse, multi-agent and protocol-level threats, interface and environment risks, and governance and autonomy concerns), reviews defenses such as prompt-injection-resistant designs, policy enforcement, sandboxing and monitoring, surveys capability and security benchmarks, and closes with open challenges including long-horizon security, adaptive attacks and human–agent security interfaces.
