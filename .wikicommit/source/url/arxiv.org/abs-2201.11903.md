## Summary

The arXiv landing page for "Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models" (Wei et al., arXiv:2201.11903), carrying the preprint's metadata and abstract. The paper introduces chain-of-thought prompting, in which a few demonstrations of intermediate reasoning steps are supplied as exemplars in the prompt, and reports that this significantly improves large language models' performance on arithmetic, commonsense and symbolic reasoning tasks, with the ability emerging naturally in sufficiently large models. Its headline result is that a 540B-parameter model given eight chain-of-thought exemplars reaches state-of-the-art accuracy on GSM8K, surpassing a finetuned GPT-3 with a verifier.
