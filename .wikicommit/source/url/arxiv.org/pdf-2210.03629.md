## Summary

"ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models" (Yao, Zhao, Yu, Du, Shafran, Narasimhan, Cao; ICLR 2023) proposes prompting a large language model to interleave free-form "thought" reasoning traces with task-specific actions in a single trajectory, and shows this combination outperforms reasoning-only and acting-only baselines on knowledge-intensive QA/fact-verification tasks (via a Wikipedia search/lookup API) and on the ALFWorld and WebShop interactive decision-making benchmarks.
