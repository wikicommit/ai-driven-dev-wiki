## Summary
A longitudinal, quantitative case study of a documented enterprise "2x" AI mandate at a mid-sized B2B software company, drawing on a panel of 802 developers and 196,212 pull requests spanning January 2024 to April 2026. Per-capita throughput reached 2.09x the pre-mandate baseline by April 2026, and a staggered difference-in-differences design links the within-developer share of that gain to AI adoption and to a further gain that grows with accumulated use, with the mandate acting as a catalyst rather than a direct driver; the gain is statistically indistinguishable across the seniority ladder but concentrated in newer repositories and not separable across frontier-model generations. Adoption also restructured code review: per-reviewer load roughly doubled, automated review overtook human review, and the human review that remained thinned toward bare approval, while merge and revert rates held steady.
