## Summary

This position paper by Rashina Hoda, accepted to the AGENT workshop at ICSE 2026, argues that agentic software engineering (SE) research should expand beyond code-centric activities toward a "whole of process" vision spanning the full software engineering lifecycle, grounded in SE's own historical evolution and emerging agentic SE frameworks. It proposes a preliminary set of CRAFT (Comprehensive, Responsible, Adaptive, Foundational, Translational) values and principles to guide the field, and offers guidance for designing consistent agentic SE terminology.
