## Summary

A comprehensive survey formalizing vibe coding as a constrained Markov Decision Process over a human-project-agent triad, proposing a taxonomy of five composable development models (Unconstrained Automation, Iterative Conversational Collaboration, Planning-Driven, Test-Driven, Context-Enhanced), and surveying the coding-LLM, agent, environment, and feedback-mechanism ecosystem, plus open challenges in security, scalable oversight, and human factors.
