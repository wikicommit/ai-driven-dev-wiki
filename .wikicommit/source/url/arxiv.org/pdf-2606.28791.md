## Summary

A position paper arguing that software engineering is shifting from authoring deterministic code toward governing probabilistic, autonomous behavior, giving rise to a distinct professional archetype the author calls the Agentic Engineer. It characterizes the shift along three axes — the unit of work moving from the function to the supervised agent workflow, correctness moving from binary assertion to statistical evaluation under uncertainty, and accountability moving from authorship to outcome ownership — and formalizes the mechanisms of autonomous software agents including reasoning-acting loops, context engineering, behavioral drift and compositional error. It deliberately sets field experiments reporting double-digit productivity gains against a randomized controlled trial reporting a 19% slowdown for experienced developers, to argue that disciplined oversight rather than raw automation is the load-bearing competency.
