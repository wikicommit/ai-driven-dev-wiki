## Summary

An argument that AI agents constitute a restructuring of what software is rather than an incremental tooling improvement: in traditional software, code carries pre-written decision logic, whereas in agentic software the agent itself is the system and its decision logic is generated at runtime. It traces a three-generation arc from licensed software to SaaS to Agent-as-a-Service, and frames Agentic Engineering as an expansion of software engineering distinguished by its core artifact, its control model and the human role. Drawing on SWE-bench Verified results and the EvoClaw benchmark — where success rates fall from over 80% on isolated tasks to at most 38% under continuous evolution — it proposes a four-stage roadmap toward self-evolving agent ecosystems.
