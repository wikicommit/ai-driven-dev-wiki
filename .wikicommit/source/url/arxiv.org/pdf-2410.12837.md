## Summary

A survey by Gupta, Ranjan and Singh tracing retrieval-augmented generation from foundational concepts to the state of the art: its basic architecture, technological advances in retrieval-augmented language models, applications across question-answering, summarization and knowledge-based tasks, recent breakthroughs in retrieval efficiency, and open challenges of scalability, bias and ethics. It is a general-purpose treatment rather than a software-engineering one, and is recorded here for the evolution account and the open challenges.
