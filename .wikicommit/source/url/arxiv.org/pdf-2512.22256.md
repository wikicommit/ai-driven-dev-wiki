## Summary

A systematic survey of 242 papers, collected from October 2023 to May 2026, on LLM-based agentic software issue resolution. It models the task as five logical phases -- repo preprocessing, localization, repair, patch validation and patch selection -- and builds taxonomies across three dimensions: benchmarks, techniques and empirical studies. It documents a paradigm shift from prompt-engineered scaffold design toward training domain-specific models, with reinforcement learning used by 24 of the 39 representative models released between February 2025 and May 2026, and argues that reported resolution rates are bounded by weak test suites, data contamination and unreliable trajectories.
