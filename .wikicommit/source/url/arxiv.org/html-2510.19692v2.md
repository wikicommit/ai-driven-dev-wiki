## Summary

Rashina Hoda's vision paper, accepted to the AGENT workshop at ICSE 2026, arguing that emerging visions of agentic software engineering are too narrowly focused on coding and should be scoped to a 'whole of process' vision spanning ethical alignment, requirements engineering, design, development and operations. It proposes a preliminary set of CRAFT values and principles and five considerations for designing agentic SE vocabulary.
