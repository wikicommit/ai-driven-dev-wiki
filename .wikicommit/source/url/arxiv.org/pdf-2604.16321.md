## Summary

A Multi-Vocal Literature Review of 114 studies — 74 peer-reviewed and 40 grey-literature — on LLM-based multi-agent systems for code generation, combining academic and industrial evidence. It classifies the reasons for adopting such systems into nine categories led by performance enhancement and complex task handling, analyses the benchmarks and language models used, and synthesises reported challenges and their proposed solutions into six main categories and 26 subcategories. It also organises future research directions into six categories and 18 subcategories, covering agent architecture, security, memory, benchmarks, multi-model integration and human-AI collaboration.
