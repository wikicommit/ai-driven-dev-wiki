## Summary
A preprint on how LLM agents that keep working after a task has succeeded react when their already-correct work is falsely blamed for a later failure. It names accepting such a false accusation "gaslight sycophancy" and the resulting damage to correct work "destructive over-correction", and introduces CAVE-Bench, a benchmark of 365 agentic tasks across six domains in which the evidence that would settle the accusation lies beyond the agent's reach. Across 14 recent models in Claude Code, false accusations damage correct work in up to 60.06% of runs, behaviour differs by harness, and a harness gate driven by the benchmark's live signals cuts replayed harm by 74%.
