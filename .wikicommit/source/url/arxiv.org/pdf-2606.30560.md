## Summary

This paper introduces TraceLab, a released trace of roughly 4,300 real coding-agent sessions (about 350,000 LLM steps and 430,000 tool calls) collected from 43 developers' day-to-day use of Claude Code and Codex, and analyses it from an LLM-serving perspective. It finds long autonomous loops, long contexts with short outputs, heavily tailed tool-call counts and latencies, and a high but imperfect prefix-cache hit rate whose misses cluster around human-paced gaps, and it derives serving-system optimisation opportunities from these observations.
