## Summary
The arXiv abstract page for SkillsBench, a benchmark of 87 tasks across 8 domains, each paired with curated Agent Skills and deterministic verifiers, used to measure whether Skills actually help LLM agents. Across 18 model-harness configurations, curated Skills raise the average pass rate from 33.9% to 50.5%, focused Skills with at most three modules outperform larger bundles, and smaller models with Skills can match larger models without them.
