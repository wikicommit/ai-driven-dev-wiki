## Summary

An observational study of 20,574 real-world coding-agent sessions from 1,639 repositories, spanning IDE and CLI workflows, which operationalizes developer-agent misalignment as a breakdown made visible through developer pushback. The authors annotate 16,118 evidence-grounded episodes along four axes — symptom, cause, outcome and resolution — identifying seven recurring symptom categories and seven cause categories. They report that 90.50% of episodes impose effort and trust costs rather than irreversible system damage, yet 91.49% of visible resolutions still require explicit developer correction.
