## Summary
A practical implementation report on introducing Spec-Driven Development with AI agents into a Software Development Project-Based Learning course for third-year undergraduates at Osaka Institute of Technology, using a four-phase workflow of investigation, planning, implementation and review together with a distributed template project carrying custom instructions. The authors analysed self-reported AI usage per phase, added lines of code per student per lecture across the 2022-2025 academic years, and instructor-assessed code comprehension. AI usage varied widely by team and phase, implementation throughput was significantly higher in 2024-2025 than in 2022-2023, and although students who used no AI showed consistently high comprehension, no statistically significant relationship between AI usage level and comprehension was found - which the authors attribute in part to weekly one-on-one instructor interviews and timely intervention.
