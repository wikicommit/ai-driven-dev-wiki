## Summary

A preliminary MSR '26 mining study by Seyedmoein Mohsenimofidi, Matthias Galster, Christoph Treude and Sebastian Baltes on AI context files as a new kind of software artifact, notable for being the first holistic empirical study of context files across tools that its authors are aware of. Its findings are the useful counterweight to vendor accounts of AGENTS.md: only 5% of 10,000 mature GitHub repositories had adopted any of the four formats scanned, there is no established content structure in the 155 AGENTS.md files examined, writing style splits five ways from descriptive to conditional, and half the files had never been revised at all. Its framing contribution is the argument that versioned context files are a rare chance to observe real-world context engineering directly, since prompts are so rarely preserved.

The four co-authors did not become pages: each is named only as a co-author with no independent facts about them in the source, which is an incidental mention rather than an independent subject.
