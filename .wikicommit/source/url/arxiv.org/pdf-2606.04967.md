## Summary

A comparative study of six frameworks that structure work around AI coding agents — GitHub Spec Kit, OpenSpec, BMAD Method, Get Shit Done, Spec Kitty and Reversa — proposing a six-dimension process taxonomy (specification, context, roles, execution, validation, portability) with a three-point scoring rubric. It reports convergence in mechanism, with the isolated prompt losing centrality to persistent artifacts, work contracts, traceability and human review, alongside a structural trade-off: no framework scores strongly on all six dimensions, and the most portable ones sacrifice roles and validation. Scores are the author's judgement from official documentation, not independent measurement.
