A review paper proposing a formal taxonomy contrasting vibe coding and agentic coding across conceptual foundations, execution architecture, workflow, safety mechanisms, and tool ecosystems, arguing the two paradigms are complementary and that hybrid workflows will define the next generation of AI-assisted software development.
