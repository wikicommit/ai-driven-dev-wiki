## Summary

A systematic survey of LLM-based issue resolution in software engineering, synthesising 175 papers and online resources under a three-part taxonomy of data, methods and analysis. It covers evaluation and training dataset construction (automated collection and synthesis), training-free frameworks (single-agent, multi-agent and workflow architectures, plus tool, memory and inference-time-scaling modules) and training-based methods (supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning), then reviews empirical critiques of benchmark data quality and agent behaviour. It closes with challenges including computational overhead, the absence of efficiency-aware evaluation, benchmark leakage and contamination, and the lack of fine-grained rewards.
