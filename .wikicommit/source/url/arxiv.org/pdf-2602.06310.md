## Summary

This vision paper by Aleti, Hoda, Ray, and Chen examines what qualifies an AI coding agent as an "agentic engineer" — proposing four conditions spanning task scope beyond coding, demonstrated agency, team collaboration, and ethical accountability — and argues that trustworthiness of such agents is a property of the system rather than merely subjective human trust. It introduces a multidimensional trustworthiness framework (technical quality, transparency and accountability, epistemic humility, and societal/ethical alignment) and proposes a shift from artefact-centric to evidence-centric inspection, with implications for rethinking verification and validation and for reframing code review as continuous, runtime monitoring in human-AI software engineering teams.
