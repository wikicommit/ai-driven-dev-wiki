## Summary

This 2026 paper presents Refute-or-Promote, an inference-time multi-agent review pattern for LLM-assisted defect discovery that combines Stratified Context Hunting, adversarial agents mandated to disprove candidates at each promotion gate, context asymmetry with cold-start reviewers, and a cross-model critic. In a 31-day campaign across seven targets it killed roughly 79% of 171 candidates before disclosure and yielded externally accepted outcomes including four CVEs, while its most instructive failure — ten reviewers unanimously endorsing a non-existent padding oracle — motivated a mandatory empirical gate.
