## Summary

A systematic grey literature review of 30 September 2025 by Ahmed Fawzy, Amjed Tahir and Kelly Blincoe, coding firsthand practitioner accounts of vibe coding into four distributions: motivations, experiences, perceptions of code quality, and QA practices. Its findings are a speed-quality trade-off paradox (Speed & Efficiency 62% of motivation units, "Fast but Flawed" 68% of code-quality units), a QA crisis (Skipped QA 36% of QA-practice units, Uncritical Trust 18%, Delegated QA to AI 10%), and a new class of vulnerable developers who can build an application but not diagnose it. The authors state the sample is self-selected and the findings not generalizable, which both generated pages record.
