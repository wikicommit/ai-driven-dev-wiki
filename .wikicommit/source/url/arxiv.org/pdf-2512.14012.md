## Summary

Through 13 field observations and a qualitative survey of 99 experienced developers, this paper studies how professional software developers used AI coding agents in 2025. It finds that they do not vibe code but control agents through planning, small-step execution, explicit context and review, because they value software quality. Developers found agents suitable for straightforward, repetitive and scaffolding tasks but not for complex tasks or business logic, and enjoyed working with agents as collaborators rather than full delegates.
