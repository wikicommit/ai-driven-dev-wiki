## Summary

DORA's State of AI-assisted Software Development 2025, drawing on a global survey of nearly 5,000 technology professionals conducted between 13 June and 21 July 2025 plus more than 100 hours of qualitative data, credited to fourteen authors. Its central claim is that AI is an amplifier, magnifying the strengths of high-performing organizations and the dysfunctions of struggling ones, so the greatest returns come from the underlying organizational system rather than the tools. It reports 90% AI adoption against 30% reporting little to no trust in AI-generated code, records that AI adoption now improves delivery throughput while still increasing delivery instability, and introduces the DORA AI Capabilities Model of seven amplifying capabilities alongside seven team archetypes, a platform engineering chapter (90% adoption) and a value stream management chapter presenting VSM as the force multiplier for AI investment. All figures are that one self-reported survey's estimates, framed by the report itself as comparisons rather than measured effects, which the generated pages state.
