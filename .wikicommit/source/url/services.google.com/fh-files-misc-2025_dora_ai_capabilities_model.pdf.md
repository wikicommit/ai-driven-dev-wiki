## Summary

Google's DORA team published the DORA AI Capabilities Model (v. 2025.1) as a practical companion to its 2025 State of AI-assisted Software Development report, based on survey responses from nearly 5,000 technology professionals and over 100 hours of qualitative data. Its central claim is that AI acts as an amplifier of an organization's existing strengths and dysfunctions, and it identifies seven capabilities that amplify AI's positive impact — a clear and communicated AI stance, healthy data ecosystems, AI-accessible internal data, strong version control practices, working in small batches, user-centric focus, and quality internal platforms — with implementation, measurement and prioritization guidance (team profiles, value stream mapping, and a prioritization workshop).
