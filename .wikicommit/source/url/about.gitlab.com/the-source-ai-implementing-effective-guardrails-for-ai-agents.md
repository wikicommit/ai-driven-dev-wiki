## Summary
A SELF (Software Engineering Leadership Forum) article, based on interviews with 54 DevSecOps practitioners and leaders, surveys the guardrails organizations need when adopting AI agents in development workflows. It groups these into four categories — user roles and access, limits and controls, customization, and logging/tracking/transparency — covering audit trails, infrastructure protection, license compliance, and production data security.
