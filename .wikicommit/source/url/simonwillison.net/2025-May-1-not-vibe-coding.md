## Summary

Simon Willison argues that two forthcoming books — *Vibe Coding* by Gene Kim and Steve Yegge (IT Revolution) and *Vibe Coding: The Future of Programming* by Addy Osmani (O'Reilly Media) — misuse the term "vibe coding", which Andrej Karpathy coined on 6 February 2025 to mean generating code with AI without caring about the code produced, an approach Karpathy framed as suited to throwaway weekend projects. Willison contends the term properly names a path for people who are not software developers, not a synonym for professional AI-assisted programming, and regrets that the resulting semantic diffusion leaves no term for the book he thinks genuinely needs writing.
