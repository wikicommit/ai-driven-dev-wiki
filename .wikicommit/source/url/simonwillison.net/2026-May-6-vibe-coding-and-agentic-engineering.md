## Summary

Highlights from a podcast conversation in which Simon Willison records that the line he drew between vibe coding and agentic engineering has blurred in his own work: as agents grew more reliable he stopped reviewing every line, including for production code. He reconciles this by treating agent output like another team's internal service, while naming why the analogy is imperfect — an agent has no professional reputation and cannot take accountability — and identifies the normalization of deviance as the risk. He also argues that evaluating software has become harder, since a polished-looking repository can now be produced in half an hour, and proposes sustained usage as the substitute signal.
