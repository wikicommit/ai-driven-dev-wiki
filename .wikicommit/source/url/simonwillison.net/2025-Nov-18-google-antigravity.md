## Summary

A link-blog post by Simon Willison on the November 18, 2025 release of Google Antigravity, a desktop IDE for agentic coding against Google's Gemini models. It points out the application's three surfaces (an agent manager dashboard, a VS Code-style editor and browser integration through a Chrome extension) and its automatically generated Markdown "artifacts", and reports that the author's first attempt to use it ended in a model-provider overload error.
