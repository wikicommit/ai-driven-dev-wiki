## Summary

Slides from a talk at the AI-Driven Development Conference 2025 Autumn arguing that AI coding tools concentrate on the implementation phase of the development cycle and still struggle with complex development, output control and knowledge management, and that spec-driven development addresses these best when it is extended upstream into requirements work. The deck proposes designing AI agents for upstream phases around context, accumulated memory such as specifications, and expected outputs — recording the why and why-not behind each requirement, using question-asking or past-project agents to fill in context, and using sub-agents that draft screen-transition diagrams and mock-ups to speed up agreement among stakeholders.
