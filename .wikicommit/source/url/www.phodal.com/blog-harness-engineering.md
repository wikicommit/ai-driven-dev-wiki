## Summary

Phodal Huang's March 2026 post argues that the problems teams hit when AI writes code at scale lie in software engineering systems built for human developers rather than in the models, and presents Harness Engineering as the practice of turning a human-first system into an agent-aware one. It organises the practice into three capabilities — making the system legible to AI, defending architectural boundaries with automated constraints, and building fast feedback loops — and illustrates them with Stripe's agent execution loop and the multi-agent platform Routa.js.
