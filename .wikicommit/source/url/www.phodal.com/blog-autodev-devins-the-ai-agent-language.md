## Summary

Phodal Huang's March 2024 post introduces DevIns (Development Instruction), an AI agent language for AutoDev that sits between natural language and instruction text: users describe development tasks in natural language with instructions such as /write, /run, /patch and /commit, and AutoDev compiles them with context into instruction text for agents and the IDE. The post explains the motivation, how to use it with the AutoDev 1.7.2 plugin, why it was renamed from DevIn, and planned next steps such as a cross-platform DevIns compiler.
