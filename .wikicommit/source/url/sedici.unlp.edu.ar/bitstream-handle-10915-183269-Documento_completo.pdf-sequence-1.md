## Summary

A Spanish-language specialist's final thesis in software engineering (UNLP, April 2025) that conducts a narrative review of 30 academic articles published 2022-2025 on integrating AI into the software development life cycle. It classifies AI tools by SDLC phase, groups adoption barriers into technical (data quality, legacy integration, over-reliance on code generators), organizational (resistance to change, lack of training and management support) and ethical (privacy, transparency) dimensions, identifies planning, testing and deployment/maintenance as the most critical phases, and proposes technical, organizational and ethical strategies.
