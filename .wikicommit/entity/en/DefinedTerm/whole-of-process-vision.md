The 'whole of process' vision is a preliminary framework proposed in [[ScholarlyArticle/toward-agentic-software-engineering-beyond-code]] for organizing agentic software engineering (SE) around the entire SE lifecycle rather than coding activity alone. It extends across five high-level core areas, approached iteratively by human and agent actors — single agents or multi-agent systems — collaborating in activities at varying levels of AI agency, depicted as a slider kept under human control. The paper presents it as a preliminary 'first look' rather than a definitive agentic SE process model.

## Usage

The vision covers five areas, each spanning associated activities, actors, and artefacts (AAA): **Agentic SE Ethical Alignment** — alignment with safe AI laws and regulations at both the micro (project, company) and macro (domain, region, country, international) levels, introduced as a new, first and central area given AI's increasing capabilities, agency, and potential for wide-scale societal impact. **Agentic Requirements Engineering** — software requirements concerns such as customer, user, and subject-matter-expert input, quality requirements, and cascading ethical requirements. **Agentic Design** — software design concerns such as architecture, UI, UX, and design for human benefit and equity. **Agentic Development** — software code design, production, testing, reviewing, and repairing, the area the paper says already has a head-start through emerging visions and datasets such as [[DefinedTerm/structured-agentic-software-engineering]] and [[Dataset/aidev]]. **Agentic Operations** — deployment, maintenance, and operations (e.g. Ops/DevOps/DevSecOps). The paper notes these area descriptions are high-level and will need to be fleshed out in collaboration with area experts, including relevant research questions, example scenarios, and specific challenges.
