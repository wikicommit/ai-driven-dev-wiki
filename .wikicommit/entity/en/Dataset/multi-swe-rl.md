Multi-SWE-RL is an open-source community launched to build large-scale reinforcement learning
training datasets for issue-resolving tasks, together with the data it has released. It is
announced in
[[ScholarlyArticle/multi-swe-bench-a-multilingual-benchmark-for-issue-resolving]], as the second of
that paper's two contributions alongside the [[Dataset/multi-swe-bench]] evaluation benchmark.

## Contents

The initial release is a set of 4,723 well-structured instances spanning seven programming
languages, released as an initial contribution which the authors state lays a solid foundation for
reinforcement learning research in this domain.

## Provenance

Alongside the released instances the authors state that they open-source their entire data
production pipeline, along with detailed tutorials, encouraging the open-source community to
continuously contribute and expand the dataset. Multi-SWE-RL is described as a community the authors
launch as well as data they publish, and they close by envisioning the ever-growing Multi-SWE-RL
community, together with [[Dataset/multi-swe-bench]], as catalysts for advancing reinforcement
learning toward its full potential.
