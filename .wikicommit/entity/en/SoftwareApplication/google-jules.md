Google Jules is an autonomous, task-oriented coding agent that can sandbox a repository, propose diffs, and execute verified changes on real projects.

## Adoption & Ecosystem

A survey on AI agentic programming classifies Google Jules, in its comparative taxonomy, as a "Task-oriented" agent that is proactive, multi-turn, tool-using, and adaptive.

[[ScholarlyArticle/agentic-software-engineering-foundational-pillars]] notes that Google Jules has included a planning step from its inception, contrasting it with Anthropic's [[SoftwareApplication/claude-code]], which only more recently added an on-demand planning mode.
