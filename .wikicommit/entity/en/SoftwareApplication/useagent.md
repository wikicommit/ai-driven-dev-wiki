USEagent (unified software engineering agent) is an AI agent proposed by Applis et al. (2025) and described in [[ScholarlyArticle/toward-agentic-software-engineering-beyond-code]] as having capabilities to handle multiple programming tasks. It is framed as "a first draft of a future AI Software Engineer which can be a team member in future software development teams involving both AI and humans."

## Capabilities

Four capabilities are named for USEagent: CodeRetrieval, ExecuteTests, EditCode, and ReviewPatch.
