Vouch is a project by Mitchell Hashimoto, hosted on GitHub, that implements an explicit trust
management system for open-source projects in which contributors must be vouched for by trusted
maintainers before they can participate. GitHub's Director of Open Source Programs cites it in
[[BlogPosting/welcome-to-the-eternal-september-of-open-source]] as an interesting example of the
community building its own responses to the flood of low-effort contributions described as open
source's [[DefinedTerm/eternal-september]], while noting that it is experimental and that some aspects
of it will be debated.

## Adoption & Ecosystem

The post places Vouch in a longer lineage of trust mechanisms in open source — Advogato's trust metric,
Drupal's credit system and the Linux kernel's `Signed-off-by` chain — and alongside other community
approaches such as invitation-only workflows and custom GitHub Actions for contributor triage and
reputation scoring.
