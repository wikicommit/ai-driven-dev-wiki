Continue.dev is an AI coding assistant that retrieves relevant context through embedding-based local recall over a developer's recent edits, rather than maintaining a persistent, cross-session memory store.

## Capabilities

A survey on AI agentic programming reports that Continue.dev can run with GPT-4 or Claude as its underlying model, with a 32,000-token default context window and no persistent memory, using embedding-based local recall over recent edits as its context-management mechanism.
