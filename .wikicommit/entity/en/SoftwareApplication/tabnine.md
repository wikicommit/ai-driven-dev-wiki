Tabnine takes a privacy-first approach to AI code completion by deploying smaller local models trained on permissively licensed code, rather than sending a developer's code to a remote cloud service — an approach the source describes as attractive to organizations and developers who do not want their code to leave their own machines.

## Adoption & Ecosystem

A survey on AI agentic programming cites Tabnine as an example of a "static" agent that follows a predefined workflow rather than adapting its behavior from feedback, and classifies it, in its comparative taxonomy, as a reactive "IDE Assistant" with no multi-turn interaction, tool use, or adaptivity.
