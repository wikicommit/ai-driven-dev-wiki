Kevin Ryan is the author of [[Book/spec-driven-development-ai-native-software-engineering]], whose first edition is dated 2026 and whose author's note is signed February 2026. He argues that the tools of AI-assisted development have leapt ahead of the practices around them, and proposes Specification Driven Development as the correction: writing specifications rather than prompting and fixing, so that the specification becomes the maintained artifact and code becomes a side effect.

## Background

Ryan refers to running engagements with teams over the preceding year, and reports that in every one, teams self-assessed at least one level higher than their actual practice on [[DefinedTerm/five-levels-of-vibe-coding]] — thinking they were Level 3 when they were Level 2. He treats that gap as itself diagnostic, on the reasoning that a team that cannot accurately measure its own AI maturity cannot plan where it is going.

He is explicit that he does not treat the methodology as his own to gatekeep, writing that the ideas draw on work happening across the industry, from individual practitioners discovering the same patterns to companies rebuilding their entire engineering culture around specification. He states that the methodology is still young, that he does not have all the answers and nobody does yet, and invites case studies, war stories, practitioner experience and technical disagreement — "including the parts you think are wrong." He frames his motivation in terms of the transition rather than the tooling: he wants the engineering community to handle the shift well, and the practices developed now to be ones practitioners are proud of in five years.

## Works & Achievements

His stated work is the book itself, which he writes in the open as an early beta and living document: the specifications, toolchain, CI/CD pipeline and provenance tracking live in the same repository as the prose, every chapter is generated from a spec, and he fixes the spec rather than the output when something does not work — making the book its own case study. The repository is on GitHub, contributions are handled as in any open-source project, and anyone who contributes direct effort is credited as a contributor. He gives his contact details as `kevin@kevinryan.io` and the website `kevinryan.io`.
