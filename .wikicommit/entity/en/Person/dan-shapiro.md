Dan Shapiro is the CEO of Glowforge and the author of [[DefinedTerm/five-levels-of-vibe-coding]], a framework grading AI-assisted development practice from Level 0 to Level 5. [[Person/kevin-ryan]] describes it as more diagnostically useful than anything else in the discourse.

## Background

Shapiro places himself at Level 4 of his own framework — "The PM": you write a spec, you argue with the AI about the spec, you plan schedules and review plans, then you leave for twelve hours and check whether the tests pass. His estimate of where everyone else sits is less flattering: he puts 90% of self-described "AI-native" developers at Level 2, pairing with the AI in a flow state and more productive than they have ever been, but well short of where they believe themselves to be.

## Works & Achievements

[[Person/kevin-ryan]] records that Shapiro published the Five Levels of Vibe Coding in January 2026, modelling it on the NHTSA's five levels of driving automation. The scale runs from Level 0 ("Spicy Autocomplete", where not a character hits the disk without approval) through Level 3 ("The Manager", where a coding agent runs continuously and the human's day is spent reviewing diffs) to Level 5, the [[DefinedTerm/dark-factory]] — named after the Fanuc robot factory, dark because humans are neither needed nor welcome, and a level the source says a handful of people on the planet operate at.
