Birgitta Böckeler is a Distinguished Engineer and AI-assisted delivery expert at Thoughtworks, with over 20 years of experience as a software developer, architect and technical leader.

## Background

She contributes to "Exploring Gen AI," a martinfowler.com series capturing Thoughtworks technologists' explorations of using generative AI for software development.

## Works & Achievements

Her memo [[BlogPosting/the-role-of-developer-skills-in-agentic-coding]], published on 25 March 2025, catalogues concrete cases where she had to intervene while using the agentic modes in Cursor, Windsurf, and Cline — almost exclusively on existing codebases rather than greenfield projects — and organises them by [[DefinedTerm/impact-radius]]. She reports that her long programming experience mattered most in the outermost category, long-term maintainability, where problems such as brittle duplicated tests and unnecessary complexity surface only weeks or months later.

The memo's forecast is deliberately calibrated: she writes that AI will not autonomously write 90% of code within a year, while allowing it may assist in writing that share for some teams and codebases, and reports it assisting her in 80% of cases in a moderately complex 15K-LOC codebase.
