## Works & Achievements

In that presentation, Gupta sets out spec-driven development through an extended analogy between building a backyard and building software, maps each element of the construction process onto a software development lifecycle, and gives a four-stage spec-driven SDLC of Research, Standardize, Define and Loop. He points at his own worked example — an agentic tic-tac-toe project whose `.claude/skills/` directory he cites as a demonstration of capturing engineering patterns as reusable [[DefinedTerm/agent-skills]] — and closes on the claim that AI amplifies what an organization already has, so the thing to get right is what is being amplified.
