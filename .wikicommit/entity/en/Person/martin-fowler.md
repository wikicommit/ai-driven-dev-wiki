Martin Fowler writes about software development at martinfowler.com. He describes creating neologisms for the things he sees in the field as a habit of his, and one common among writers in it, on the grounds that software development "still lacks much useful jargon."

## Background

The sources here say little about Fowler beyond that writing practice. In [[BlogPosting/semantic-diffusion]] he presents himself as an observer of how software terminology decays in use, writing in 2006 that he was watching "agile" and "Web 2.0" lose their definitions publicly, and that he had seen the same happen earlier to "object-oriented" and to "patterns" before both recovered. He also argues that those identified as the originators of a term carry a responsibility both to keep re-articulating its meaning and to acknowledge that the ideas behind it evolve.

## Works & Achievements

Fowler coined [[DefinedTerm/semantic-diffusion]] in [[BlogPosting/semantic-diffusion]], dated 14 December 2006 — an attribution [[Person/simon-willison]] repeated in March 2025 when he invoked the term to describe what was then happening to the definition of [[DefinedTerm/vibe-coding]]. The same post points to "RefactoringMalapropism" as a more personal case of the effect, which he sets aside there.
