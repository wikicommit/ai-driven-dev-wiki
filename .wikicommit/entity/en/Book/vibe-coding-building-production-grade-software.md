"Vibe Coding: Building Production-Grade Software With GenAI, Chat, Agents, and Beyond" is a book by
Gene Kim and Steve Yegge, published by IT Revolution. In early May 2025 it was forthcoming, with its
cover art already designed.

Its title and subtitle drew criticism from Simon Willison in
[[BlogPosting/two-publishers-and-three-authors-fail-to-understand-what-vibe-coding-means]]. Willison
argues that the subtitle describes "exactly what vibe coding is not": in his reading of Andrej
Karpathy's original description, [[DefinedTerm/vibe-coding]] means generating code with AI without
caring about the code produced, as a way to build throwaway projects, rather than building
production-grade software.

## Characters & Publication

Published by IT Revolution. Willison discussed it alongside Addy Osmani's O'Reilly book, which was
originally announced as "Vibe Coding: The Future of Programming" and later retitled
[[Book/beyond-vibe-coding]].
