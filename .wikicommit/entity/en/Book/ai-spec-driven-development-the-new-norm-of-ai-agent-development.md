*AI仕様駆動開発: AIエージェント開発の新常識* ("AI spec-driven development: the new common sense of
AI agent development") is a Japanese technical book by 岡崎 太, published as a Kindle-only title in the
AI Zamurai Tech Series. It gives a compact, systematic account of the spec-driven approach to
development with AI agents — what role a specification plays when an agent writes the code, and the
practical steps for working that way — and, as one title in that series, it limits itself to material
directly applicable to practice.

A review of the book on note.com places it in the wave of interest in
[[DefinedTerm/spec-driven-development]] that followed Amazon's announcement of
[[SoftwareApplication/kiro]] and the "80% design, 20% development" approach that KDDI-affiliated KAG
brought into practice in the second half of 2025.

## Content

According to the review, the theme running through the book is a division of labour in writing the
specification itself: the human thinks through the core of the requirements, and the AI fleshes out the
specification around them, so that writing a specification becomes a joint task with the AI. The
reviewer singles out the book's point that an AI writes far better code from a clear specification than
from a vague instruction — a matter, in the reviewer's reading, of how the input is structured rather
than of model capability, because tacit knowledge in a person's head has to be externalized as a
specification before the AI can use it.

The reviewer also credits the book with lowering the entry barrier for engineers who do not know what to
put in a specification, while noting that basic design skill still has to be developed separately, and
describes what a reader takes away as a concrete picture of the order in which to work on the next
project rather than a body of theory.
