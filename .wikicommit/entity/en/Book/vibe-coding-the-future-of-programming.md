*Vibe Coding: The Future of Programming* is a book by Addy Osmani, published by O'Reilly Media. [[Person/simon-willison]] describes it as talking about how professional engineers can integrate AI-assisted coding tools into their workflow.

It is recorded here as one of the two titles Willison cites in [[BlogPosting/not-vibe-coding]] as misusing the term [[DefinedTerm/vibe-coding]], which on his reading means generating code with AI without caring about the code produced rather than professional AI-assisted development.

## Publication

Published by O'Reilly Media. In May 2025 it was available as an Early Release, marked "RAW & UNEDITED", with a cover reading "Vibe Coding: The Future of Programming — Leverage Your Experience in the Age of AI". An update to Willison's post dated 4 September 2025 records that the book had by then been retitled *Beyond Vibe Coding: From Coder to AI-Era Developer*.

## Reception

Willison criticised the original title's use of the term, arguing that the authors and publishers involved had not read to the end of the tweet in which the term was coined. He called the later retitling "a much better title".
