*Vibe Coding* is a book by Gene Kim and Steve Yegge, published by IT Revolution, carrying the subtitle "Building Production-Grade Software With GenAI, Chat, Agents, and Beyond". As of May 2025 it was forthcoming, with its cover art already designed.

It is recorded here as one of the two titles [[Person/simon-willison]] cites in [[BlogPosting/not-vibe-coding]] as misusing the term [[DefinedTerm/vibe-coding]]. On Willison's reading, building production-grade software is "exactly what vibe coding is not", since the term denotes generating code with AI without caring about the code produced.

## Publication

Published by IT Revolution. Willison's post reproduces the cover, which presents the title as "VIBE CODING: BUILDING PRODUCTION-GRADE SOFTWARE WITH GENAI, CHAT, AGENTS, AND BEYOND" by "GENE KIM & STEVE YEGGE" over a rainbow digital background. No publication date is stated in the source.

## Reception

Willison criticised the title's use of the term, arguing that the authors and publisher had not read to the end of the tweet in which the term was coined before publishing a book about it, and that the mistake would be hard to fix because the cover art was already designed.
