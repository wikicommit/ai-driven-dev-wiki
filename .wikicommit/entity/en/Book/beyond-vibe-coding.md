Beyond Vibe Coding is a book by Addy Osmani, published by O'Reilly, on AI-assisted and agentic engineering. Osmani describes its subject matter as specs, harnesses, evals, context, and shipping production-grade software with AI, and points readers to free tips on the book's companion site for those interested in the topic.

## Characters & Publication

The book is published by O'Reilly. Osmani announced its release in [[BlogPosting/your-ai-coding-agents-need-a-manager]].

The book was originally announced as "Vibe Coding: The Future of Programming", with an O'Reilly early
release. Simon Willison criticized that title in
[[BlogPosting/two-publishers-and-three-authors-fail-to-understand-what-vibe-coding-means]], arguing that
a book about how professional engineers integrate AI-assisted coding tools into their workflow is not
about [[DefinedTerm/vibe-coding]] in its original sense. In an update dated September 4, 2025, Willison
noted that the book was now titled "Beyond Vibe Coding: From Coder to AI-Era Developer", which he called
a much better title.
