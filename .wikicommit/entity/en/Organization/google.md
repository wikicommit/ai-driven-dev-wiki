Google is named as one of three companies -- alongside Anthropic and Cursor's maker, Anysphere -- that have converged on the same underlying architecture for long-running AI agents: separating the model loop, the execution sandbox, and a durable session log, and bundling memory as a managed service any agent invocation can query.

## Activities & Products

- The [[SoftwareApplication/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform]], announced at Cloud Next '26, which folded Vertex AI into a single platform for long-running, memory-backed agents.
- The Agent Development Kit (ADK), a code-first development kit for building agents on the platform, and Agent Studio, its visual counterpart.
