Microsoft is a large, globally distributed software company whose engineers span many product
domains, work across diverse codebases and program in a wide range of languages. As described in
[[ScholarlyArticle/adoption-and-impact-of-command-line-ai-coding-agents]], a study written by
Microsoft employees, it sells AI tools, encourages their use, and owns [[Organization/github]], the
maker of [[SoftwareApplication/github-copilot-cli]].

## Activities & Products

For several years before 2026, Microsoft's employees had access to [[SoftwareApplication/github-copilot]]
in non-CLI forms — code completion, chat and agent mode in IDEs such as VS Code. In the first half of
2026 its engineers had two sanctioned agentic command-line tools: Copilot CLI, available to Microsoft
through a product preview program ahead of its general availability, and
[[SoftwareApplication/claude-code]], which reached engineers along a narrower path — from
late 2025, specific divisions and individuals received access through a managed program whose access
list evolved over time. The study treats January 5, 2026 as the point around which the company's
broader internal use of these tools took off. Microsoft also ran an internal "Agentic Engineering
Day" of demonstrations, tutorials and hands-on sessions on the tools.

Shortly after April 29, 2026, an internal announcement indicated that Claude Code licenses would be
discontinued for most engineers in about a month, with affected engineers directed to transition to
Copilot CLI. In the company's telemetry-based study of the rollout, adoption of Copilot CLI spread
mainly through visible peer and manager use, and adopters of either tool merged roughly 24% more pull
requests than a synthetic counterfactual predicted.

Microsoft organises engineering roles on parallel career-stage ladders — IC for individual
contributors and M for people-managers — in which a higher number indicates greater scope and
seniority and stages with the same number on the two ladders, such as IC4 and M4, are calibrated as
peers.
