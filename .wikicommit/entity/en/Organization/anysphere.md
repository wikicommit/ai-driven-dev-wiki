Anysphere, Inc., doing business as Cursor, is an American software company that develops [[SoftwareApplication/cursor]], an AI coding agent and software development environment. Wikipedia describes the San Francisco-based company as building tools and models that let users edit code, search codebases, run commands and complete programming tasks from natural-language instructions — which places it, alongside [[Organization/anthropic]] and [[Organization/openai]], among the vendors whose product decisions shape day-to-day [[DefinedTerm/agentic-coding]] practice.

## History

The company was incorporated in 2022 by Michael Truell, Sualeh Asif, Arvid Lunnemark and Aman Sanger while they were students at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. Wikipedia lists Truell as CEO and Asif as chief product officer, with Jordan Topoleski as chief operating officer; Lunnemark is recorded as having left in October 2025 to found a safety-focused AI research lab, Integrous Research. As of August 2025 the company employed roughly 300 people.

Its funding history as recorded there runs from an $8 million seed round announced in October 2023, led by the OpenAI Startup Fund, through a $60 million Series A at a $400 million valuation, a $900 million Series C led by Thrive Capital confirmed on 5 June 2025 at a $9.9 billion post-money valuation, to a $2.3 billion Series D closed on 13 November 2025 co-led by Accel and Coatue Management at a $29.3 billion valuation with participation from Google and Nvidia. Revenue milestones are given as crossing $100 million in annual recurring revenue in January 2025, topping $500 million ARR by June 2025, exceeding $1 billion annualized after the Series D, and surpassing $3 billion ARR by early 2026.

Ownership changed hands in 2026. Wikipedia records SpaceX announcing on 21 April 2026 a deal giving it the right to acquire the company for $60 billion later that year or to pay $10 billion for joint work, exercising that right on 16 June in an all-stock deal, and closing on 14 August 2026 — with Anysphere shares converted into an aggregate 389,289,254 shares of SpaceX Class A common stock, and the company surviving as a wholly owned SpaceX subsidiary being integrated into the SpaceXAI team.

## Activities & Products

The main product is Cursor itself; Wikipedia also lists Graphite and Cursor Composer among the company's products. Its agent features are described as searching across a codebase, editing files, running terminal commands and carrying out multi-step programming tasks from natural-language instructions. Cursor 2.0, released in October 2025, added support for running multiple agents in parallel using git worktrees or remote machines, and the company has released agents for web, mobile, command-line and cloud environments. Bugbot, launched in July 2025, is a code-review tool integrated with GitHub pull requests, and team and enterprise plans offer administrative controls, usage analytics, single sign-on, model controls and compliance features. On models, the company integrates third-party large language models including models from Anthropic and OpenAI in addition to its own coding models.

Acquisitions recorded there are Supermaven, an AI code-completion startup, in November 2024 — its team folded into Cursor and its standalone product wound down in late 2025; engineering talent from Koala in July 2025 to staff a new enterprise-readiness team, without adopting Koala's product; and an agreement in December 2025 to acquire Graphite, a New York code-review startup.

Two customer-facing incidents are recorded as controversies. In April 2025 an AI help-desk agent named "Sam" invented a non-existent login policy, prompting user cancellations before staff apologised and issued refunds. In July 2025 a change to the $20 Pro plan, switching from 500 requests to a usage-metered cap, drew complaints about unexpected charges; the company apologised, rolled back the limits and said it would refund affected users.

On hiring, Wikipedia states the company prohibits the use of AI tools during the first round of coding interviews and invites finalists for a two-day on-site project with the core team — a notable position for a vendor whose own product is an AI coding tool.
