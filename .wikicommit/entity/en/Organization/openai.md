OpenAI describes itself as an AI research and deployment company, with the stated mission of
ensuring that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity. It glosses that term as AI
systems that are generally smarter than humans. Its about page leads with that mission and links
out from it to a statement of the organization's plan for AGI and to its Charter.

The organization is structured in two parts. The nonprofit OpenAI Foundation governs the for-profit
OpenAI Group, which operates as a public benefit corporation, and OpenAI states that its mission
advances through the combined impact of both. It also says that it is building safe and beneficial
AGI, while adding that it would consider its mission fulfilled if its work aided others in
achieving that outcome.

## History

OpenAI was founded in 2015 as a nonprofit. In 2019 it created a for-profit subsidiary, which it
says was done to scale its research and deployment efforts and which it states has always been
governed and controlled by the nonprofit.

Its current arrangement dates from a recapitalization announced on 28 October 2025. Under it the
nonprofit became the OpenAI Foundation and the for-profit became OpenAI Group PBC, a public
benefit corporation — a form OpenAI describes as required, unlike a conventional corporation, to
advance its stated mission and consider the broader interests of all stakeholders. OpenAI states
that the Foundation continues to control the Group and that the two share the same mission, and
says the change was completed after nearly a year of dialogue with the offices of the Attorneys
General of California and Delaware.

Governance of the Group's board runs through the Foundation, which OpenAI says holds special
voting and governance rights allowing it to appoint all of the Group's directors and replace them
at any time. Oversight of safety and security practices across the whole of OpenAI, including the
Group, sits with a Safety and Security Committee that remains a committee of the Foundation.

## Activities & Products

OpenAI presents its work under two headings, research and products. Its own product listing names
ChatGPT in its consumer, business, enterprise and education forms together with
[[SoftwareApplication/openai-codex]], and lists an API platform for developers separately from
those.
