The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) is a directed fund under the Linux Foundation, co-founded by [[Organization/anthropic]], Block and [[Organization/openai]] with support from other companies. Its relevance here is as the body that now holds [[DefinedTerm/model-context-protocol]]: Anthropic donated the protocol to the AAIF in December 2025, roughly a year after introducing it as an open standard.

## History

The Wikipedia account of MCP records the donation in December 2025 and the foundation's co-founders, and says nothing else about how or when the foundation itself was established. No further detail on its governance, membership terms or other hosted projects appears in the source ingested here.
