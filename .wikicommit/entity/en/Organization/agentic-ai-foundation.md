The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) is a directed fund under the Linux Foundation, co-founded by [[Organization/anthropic]], Block and [[Organization/openai]] with support from other companies. Its relevance here is as the body that holds two of this wiki's subjects: [[DefinedTerm/model-context-protocol]], which Anthropic donated to the AAIF in December 2025, roughly a year after introducing it as an open standard, and the [[DefinedTerm/agents-md]] context-file format.

## History

The Wikipedia account of MCP records the donation in December 2025 and the foundation's co-founders, and says nothing else about how or when the foundation itself was established. No further detail on its governance, membership terms or other hosted projects appears in the sources ingested here.

## Activities & Products

Two stewardship roles are recorded. The AAIF holds the Model Context Protocol following Anthropic's December 2025 donation. It also stewards AGENTS.md: the format's official site states that AGENTS.md "is now stewarded by the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation", and the site's copyright notice identifies AGENTS.md as "a Series of LF Projects, LLC".

One event is recorded: in April 2026 the AAIF held the MCP Dev Summit North America in New York City, drawing approximately 1,200 attendees.
