AI Agent User Group (AIAU) is a user group for coding agents. According to [[BlogPosting/ai-code-agents-matsuri-seven-techniques]], it came into being spontaneously amid the after-party of [[Event/ai-code-agents-matsuri-2025-winter]], started by the participants themselves rather than by the people promoting AI coding agents. The post's author considers that the strongest form a community can take, and describes it as still active and as contributing greatly to the spread of AI coding agents in Japan.

## Activities & Products

AIAU organized Cursor Meetup #2, with [[Organization/cyberagent]]'s team providing the venue and a guest from Cursor. That meetup focused on designers and creators as well as engineers, which the post presents as putting into practice the idea that AI coding agents are not only for people who write code.
