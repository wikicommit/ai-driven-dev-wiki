Cognition describes itself as an applied AI lab focused on reasoning. Its stated aim is to build AI
teammates with capabilities well beyond those of existing AI tools, and it presents solving reasoning
as the way to unlock possibilities across a wide range of disciplines — with code, in its words, "just
the beginning".

## History

Cognition introduced [[SoftwareApplication/devin]] in the post
[[BlogPosting/introducing-devin]] on 12 March 2024, calling it the first AI software engineer. In that
post it described itself as well funded, including a $21 million Series A led by Founders Fund, and
listed a number of industry figures as supporters. Devin was then in early access through a waitlist.

## Activities & Products

Devin, an autonomous coding agent that works with its own shell, code editor and browser in a
sandboxed compute environment, is the product the launch post centres on, and the post reports
Cognition's own evaluation of it on [[Dataset/swe-bench]].
