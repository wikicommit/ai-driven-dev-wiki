In the lead's second paragraph, the first half of the sentence "Where OpenClaw places a broker at the centre, the authors observe that Hermes answers the question of where to put the broker with no broker at all" is the page's own gloss. arXiv 2604.14228 states only the Hermes half ("the design question of where to put the broker is answered, in Hermes, with no broker at all"). It never calls OpenClaw's gateway a broker; it describes OpenClaw as a persistent daemon / gateway control plane at the system's centre. The paraphrase is reasonable, but because the sentence frames it as part of the authors' observation, a reader may take the word 'broker' for OpenClaw to be the paper's own.
