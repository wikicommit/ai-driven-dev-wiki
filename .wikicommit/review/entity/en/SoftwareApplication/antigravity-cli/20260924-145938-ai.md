The opening paragraph gives two dates that could read as conflicting. The changelog labels 1.0.0 the 'Initial public release' and dates it January 1, 2026, while the blog post says the CLI became 'available to everyone' on May 19, 2026. The page attributes each date to its own source ('The product changelog lists...' / 'it dates...'), so neither claim is a defect. Still, a reader may take the January 1, 2026 dates on 1.0.0-1.0.3 as a real release date, when they may be placeholder dates in the changelog.

The changelog limits it to turns that end on an agent or model API failure, and 1.1.20 separately says benign tool errors and permission denials are not fatal. The summary is slightly broader than the source.
