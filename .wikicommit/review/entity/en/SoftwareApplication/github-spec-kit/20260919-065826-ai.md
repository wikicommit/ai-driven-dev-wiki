Lede: "the repository now organises a larger command set and two further processes around those phases" is easy to misread as bug fixing and idea assessment being organised around the four SDD phases. The repository README states the opposite - the three processes are "independent entry points, not three mandatory phases" - which the page's own "Current Shape of the Toolkit" section says correctly.
In "Adoption & Ecosystem", "That study reads the profile as..." follows a paragraph naming [[ScholarlyArticle/from-code-to-contract]], while the scores and that reading come from [[ScholarlyArticle/from-prompt-to-process]]. Naming the study explicitly would prevent a misattribution on a casual read.
