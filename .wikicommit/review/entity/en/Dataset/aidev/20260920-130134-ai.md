Not a defect since it matches the source's own wording, but worth flagging for anyone tightening the prose later.
In the second 'Use' paragraph, the sentence blends figures from two different denominators without flagging it: 61.38% and 71.58% are computed over R_pop (all 33,596 agent-authored PRs / 39,122 comments), while the 25.92% vs 1.63% agent-steering figures are computed over R∩ (the 9,616/5,574-PR intersection subset used for the human-vs-agent comparison). Each number is individually correct per the source, but the sentence reads as one continuous statistic without noting the scope shift.

The page's broader framing is a reasonable inference from the dataset's stated scope (five agents, GitHub-only, cutoff date) but is not itself asserted in this general form by either AIDev source paper.
