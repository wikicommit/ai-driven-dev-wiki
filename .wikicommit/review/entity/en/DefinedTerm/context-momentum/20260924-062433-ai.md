The context-management sentence merges two separate behaviours from different videos: YT15 opened a new 'composer' window (a new chat thread) when it moved on to animating arrows, and YT22 closed all tabs before new phases (source lines 452-455). The quote "clear the context from the AI" belongs only to YT22's tab-closing, and 'before a new phase' describes YT22 only. The paper also does not connect this context management to context momentum. That section of the paper is on prompting strategies (3.4.2). Putting the sentence on this page could suggest a link the paper does not draw.
The positive-effects paragraph says "In another session the developer's impromptu decision...". This could be read as the same developer as in the exchange-rate example. It is not: the exchange-rate example is YT18 and the animations example is YT15, two different creators (source lines 355, 367-369). The source also says YT18 was not part of the final framework analysis (footnote 2, line 385).
