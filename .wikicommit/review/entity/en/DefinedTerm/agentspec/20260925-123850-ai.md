The closing sentence of 'When It Applies' says the rules for the main experiments were 'written by the authors'. That is accurate for the code and embodied agents, where the source says 'the rules were manually developed based on detailed risk descriptions'. For the autonomous-vehicle experiments, though, the source says it 'adopt[s] rules from FixDrive [37]' and uses 'existing predicates and predefined actions from prior work [40]'. Those are earlier papers that share some authors with this one. The page's phrasing slightly overstates how much of the rule set is new to this paper.
The invoke_action example ('adjusting a vehicle's following distance') combines two passages: the prose example 'adjusting speed or enabling hazard lights' and the follow_dist(10) enforcement in Figure 8. Both passages support it, but the source's own prose example is speed or hazard lights, not following distance.
