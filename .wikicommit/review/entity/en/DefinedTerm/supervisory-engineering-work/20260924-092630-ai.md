The Usage paragraph says the rhythm of directing, waiting and evaluating 'may help explain why flow state eroded'. The hedge 'may' comes from the source (Sec. 5.2: 'AI coding assistants may structurally undermine flow state'), but 'eroded' could be read as an established finding. In the source, the within-participant decline in flow state was not statistically significant (Table 6: p_adj = 0.195). The erosion appears only in the cohort analysis, where flow state was the main issue for 54% of the Negative cohort at Q1 and 76% at Q2. A later reader may want that qualified.
