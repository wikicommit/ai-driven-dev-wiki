The closing sentence of 'When It Applies' says the broader idea of treating a development organisation as a factory 'is not either party's alone' and supports that only by noting that the StrongDM source credits Dan Shapiro with naming the Dark Factory level of adoption. Shapiro is credited with naming an adoption level, not the broader factory idea, so the support given does not quite match the claim. A source the page already holds says more directly what the sentence is getting at: Osmani's 'Software Factories, Light and Dark' (addyosmani.com/blog-software-factories.md line 22) dates the software factory term to Bob Bemer's 1968 paper and to a half-century-old dream. As written, a reader could take Shapiro as the origin of the general idea.
