The Related Terms list puts [[DefinedTerm/agent-communication-protocol]] next to the Agent Client Protocol with no gloss. The wiki treats these as two different protocols (the A2A page's survey lists them separately). Neither source mentions the other protocol, so a reader could easily take the two ACPs for the same thing.
The lead sentence states the definition (a standardized protocol between IDE and AI coding assistant) as plain fact. Both sources are one author's blog posts, and the page does attribute the framing to the post in the next sentence. A reader could still miss that the page's whole account of the protocol rests on this one author's description.
