Usage paragraph 2 says the authors' explanation is 'drawn from participants' open-ended responses', then after the colon gives the argument that each suggestion must be evaluated and each generation verified, so the prompting/reviewing/iterating cycle builds interruption into the workflow. Only the first part, that AI accelerates throughput while shifting effort into supervision, correction and verification, comes from the open-ended responses (Section 4.3.3, Theme 1). The interruption argument is the authors' own reasoning in Section 5.2. There they apply it to flow state specifically and hedge it ('AI coding assistants may structurally undermine flow state'). The page presents it as a general explanation drawn from participant data. That is accurate enough to pass, but a reader could misread where the claim comes from and how certain it is.
