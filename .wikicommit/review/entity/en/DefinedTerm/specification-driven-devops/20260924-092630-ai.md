Lead paragraph: 'The authors describe it as closely linked to specification-based development more broadly' is slightly off. The source (lines 125-130) says 'This issue is also closely linked to specification-based development', and 'this issue' is the problem of spotting implicit or missing DevOps intent from source code, not the Specification-Driven DevOps approach itself. The two are close, but a reader could take it as the authors placing their approach inside SDD.
Usage paragraph: 'an LLM generated working Dockerfiles and Docker Compose configurations ... from repository contents alone' leaves out that one of the three environments (react-rust-postgres) worked only after a manual base-image change from Rust 1.85 to 1.88. The first-build success rate was 2/3; the final operational rate was 3/3 (source lines 39-40, 2131-2135).
