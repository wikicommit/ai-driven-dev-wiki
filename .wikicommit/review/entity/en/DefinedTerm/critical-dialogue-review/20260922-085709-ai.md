Frontmatter `description` compresses the source's hedge into an absolute: it says independence of perspective is what "same-model self-review is argued not to provide", whereas the post says only that with same-model self-review 「観点の独立性は強くありません」 (independence of perspective is *not strong*). The body prose gets this right ("gives weak independence of perspective"), so the overstatement is confined to the description field and would be worth tightening there.
The `[[DefinedTerm/llm-as-a-judge]]` gloss — "a model evaluating output, without the adjudication and re-criticism rounds that distinguish this practice" — is easy to misread. Taken as the coupled pair this page defines (producer adjudicates accept/reject/hold with a recorded reason, then the critic re-criticizes the rejections), it is accurate and the existing llm-as-a-judge page describes nothing like it. Read loosely as "judging involves no repeated rounds", it sits against that page, which describes "an independent judge paired with an implementation sub-agent in a retry loop that repeats until the work passes" and "several judges in iterative debate". Not routed as a cross-page finding because the two pages do not state the same fact differently once the phrase is read as the coupled adjudication-then-re-criticism structure.
The page's title term is an English rendering of 批判的対話(レビュー). In the source that exact compound appears as a figure caption (「Claude CodeとCodexによる批判的対話レビュー」) and in section/heading phrasing (「Claude Code × Codexの批判的対話による設計・実装レビューの仕組み」, 「AIによるレビューの批判的対話は、次の3ラウンドを最小単位にしています」); the post never presents it as a formally coined term. The page's "is the name [the post] gives to" phrasing stays on the right side of that, but a later reader should know the term's standing rests on descriptive Japanese usage, not on a coinage claim.
