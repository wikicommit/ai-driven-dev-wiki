Opening paragraph lists the team-level properties the technical harness cannot supply as 'reproducibility, auditability, transferability, review capacity and coordination'; the source (Sec. 5.2) says 'review saturation', which names the failure mode rather than the capacity. The paraphrase is close, but it moves the term away from the paper's own wording.
'When It Applies' says 'each mechanism is defined relative to one [specification]'. This generalizes a little beyond the source. The source says the harness's 'central artifact is the specification' (Sec. 5.2) and that each interaction pattern is 'an operation on the specification substrate' (Sec. 6.1). Some mechanisms, such as context engineering and persistent shared knowledge, are not themselves defined in terms of a specification.
'lists specification drift and cost growth among its risks': in the source, Table 5 / Sec. 8.2 ('Specification drift', 'Cost spiral') gives these as risks of the transition to SDD-governed ASE as a whole, not of the methodological harness specifically. Also, 'cost growth' paraphrases the paper's term 'cost spiral'.
