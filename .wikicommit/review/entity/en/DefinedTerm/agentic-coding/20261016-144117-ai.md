They are 'The supervisor role is real but narrower than "monitoring execution"' and 'What that study adds to the risk picture is a finding about who benefits'. The facts they introduce are supported by the Anthropic study: about 70% of planning decisions to people, about 80% of execution decisions to Claude, and success rising with expertise. Neither source says that its own account is narrower than the review's, or that the study qualifies the review's risk picture. A later reader could take these sentences as conclusions one of the sources draws.
