Key Points says the share of hazardous tasks 'fell to 0% in all ten unsafe categories'. In Table 4, 'Liquid and Spill Damage' was already 0% without AgentSpec, so nothing fell in that category. It would be more precise to say 0% in all ten categories with AgentSpec enabled.
The Notes sentence that calls AgentSpec 'an instance of the deterministic, pre-execution style of guardrails' is the wiki's own framing, not the paper's. The paper does describe deterministic enforcement at discrete checkpoints and checking proposed actions before they run. But AgentSpec also hooks after an action produces an observation (AgentStep), on state-change triggers, and at agent finish, so 'pre-execution' describes only part of how it works.
