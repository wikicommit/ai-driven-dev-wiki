Key Point 4 renders the paper's caveat about the 90.67% unknown-resolution share as "not a true failure rate". The source's wording is "These reflect observable within-session outcomes rather than true resolution rates, as failures are more likely reported than successes confirmed in conversational logs" — i.e. it is a resolution rate, not a failure rate, that the authors warn against reading off the number. The gloss preserves the caution's intent but swaps the quantity being cautioned about, and a reader could take the sentence to mean 90.67% of episodes are unresolved failures.
The damage-severity Key Point lists DS1 (90.50%), DS2 (8.44%) and DS3 (0.07%) but silently drops DS0 (0.08%) and DS4 Unobservable (0.91%), so the three figures given read as an exhaustive breakdown while summing to 99.01%.
