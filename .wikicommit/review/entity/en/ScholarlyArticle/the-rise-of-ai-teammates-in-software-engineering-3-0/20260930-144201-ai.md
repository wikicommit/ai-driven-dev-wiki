The linked page Dataset/aidev.md describes AIDev as 932,791 PRs across 116,211 repositories and 72,189 developers with an August 1, 2025 cutoff and a >100-star curated subset of 33,596 PRs, while this page (correctly per its source, lines 136-137, 286, 290-294) gives 456,535 PRs, 61,453 repositories, 47,303 developers, a June 22, 2025 cutoff and a >=500-star AIDev-pop subset of 7,122 PRs. These are different releases of a dataset the paper calls a 'living, extensible resource', so neither page is wrong, and this page frames its figures as 'As the paper describes it'. A reader moving between the two pages could still take the numbers as conflicting, and the AIDev page does not mention that there was an earlier, smaller release.
The paper's own finding (source line 157) rounds GitHub Copilot's median job time to 'within 13 minutes', but the page uses the more precise 12.8 minutes from Finding #4 (line 495). Both figures are supported. This note only records which of the two the page followed.
