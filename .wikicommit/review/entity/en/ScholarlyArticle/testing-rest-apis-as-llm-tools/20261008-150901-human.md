I was a bit curious about why the study focuses specifically on REST APIs. I understand the advantages of leveraging OpenAPI specifications and existing API testing methods, but agents also use other types of tools, such as CLIs and file operations. It would have been interesting to see a clearer distinction between the parts of the approach that are specific to REST APIs and those that could be applied to other tools.

I also think this evaluation approach could be useful for tools exposed through MCP.
