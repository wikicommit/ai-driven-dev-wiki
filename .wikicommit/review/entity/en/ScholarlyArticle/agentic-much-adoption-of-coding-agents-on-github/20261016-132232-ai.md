The Key Points bullet says "12.08% of the projects showed coding agent traces at the file level … and a further 11.51% only at the commit level, giving a conservative adoption estimate of 22.20%". The two percentages have different denominators. 12.08% is of all 128,018 projects. 11.51% is of the 112,551 projects with no file-level trace (12,951 projects). Added together they do not give 22.20%, which is (15,467 + 12,951) / 128,018. The paper's introduction words it the same way, so the page is faithful to it. A reader could still take the two figures as shares of the same population and expect them to sum to 22.20%.
The bullet on 23.97% Pervasive calls this group "file-level adopters with at least 10 post-adoption commits and some AI-assisted commits". In the source's Table 4 that figure is the "All Files with commits" row. That row counts projects detected through .gitignore as well, so it is slightly broader than "file-level" in the narrow sense. The page's earlier definition of file-level adoption does include .gitignore-only projects, so the wording is consistent within the page.
