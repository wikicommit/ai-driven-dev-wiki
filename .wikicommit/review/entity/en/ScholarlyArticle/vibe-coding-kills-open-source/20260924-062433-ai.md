Key Points bullet 3 ends with 'This result is derived from the paper's theoretical model rather than measured.' The source (the arXiv abstract page, lines 29) supports the first half: the result is presented as the output of 'a model with endogenous entry and heterogeneous project quality'. It does not say the paper has no empirical or measured component. So 'rather than measured' is an inference from what the abstract leaves out, not something the source says. It reads as a hedge rather than a new fact, but a later reviewer with the full PDF may want to confirm it.
properties.description says the paper argues vibe coding 'can reduce the availability and quality of open-source software unless maintainers are paid differently'. That merges two separate statements in the abstract: (a) the harm result, which holds 'when OSS is monetized only through direct user engagement', and (b) the conclusion that sustaining OSS 'at its current scale' needs major changes in how maintainers are paid. The body keeps these two apart correctly. The one-line description drops both qualifiers.
