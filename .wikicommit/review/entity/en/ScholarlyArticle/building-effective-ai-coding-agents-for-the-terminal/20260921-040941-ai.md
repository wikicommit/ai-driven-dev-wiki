Key Points states "six thinking depth levels" as one of the system's empirically-tuned constants. The source says exactly that in its Discussion and Conclusion, so the claim is supported — but the same paper contradicts itself: Section 2.2.6 says "Four configurable depth levels (OFF, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH)" and the appendix constants table records 4. The page reproduces the Discussion figure without noting the paper's own inconsistency.
The 54% figure appears twice unqualified on the page. That matches the Conclusion verbatim, but Section 2.3.6 states it more narrowly — "peak context consumption of observations" — and attributes it to "quantitative evaluations". The Notes section reframes it as a measurement in the author's own use; that framing is defensible from Section 5's admission that the paper lacks systematic quantitative evaluation, but it is the page's characterisation, not the source's wording for this number.
