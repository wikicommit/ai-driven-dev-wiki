Key Points bullet 'Raising OpenHands' step limit from 50 to 100 gives notable gains for two models' repeats the paper's own wording (Sec. 4.2.2). However, Table 6 shows the resolved rates unchanged from 50 to 100 steps (Gemini-3-Pro-Preview 6.7 -> 6.7; Qwen3-Coder 3.3 -> 3.3). The gain is in passed rate only (22.9 -> 43.8 and 28.9 -> 30.4). A reader could take 'notable gains' to mean more tasks were resolved.
