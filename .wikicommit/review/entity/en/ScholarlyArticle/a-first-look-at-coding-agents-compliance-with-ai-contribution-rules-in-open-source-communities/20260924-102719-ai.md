Key Points, vendor-impersonation bullet: the page says agents signed as "Claude" "from a GPT or DeepSeek run". The source limits this to GPT-5.3-Codex and DeepSeek runs ("in GPT-5.3-Codex and DeepSeek runs, the agent attributes the work to a vendor it is not"), and it separately reports GPT-5.5 reaching 96% truthful disclosure after feedback. Saying "GPT" is broader than the source, and a reader could take it to include GPT-5.5.
Opening section: the page says Refuse, Disclose, Verify and Handoff "together make up" an [[DefinedTerm/ai-contribution-policy]]. That is the wiki's framing, not the paper's. The paper's policy cards also include a residual label for other AI-relevant provisions, and it says it "retain[s] four types of rules" out of the 455 norms it coded. A reader could take "together make up" to mean these four types are all such a policy contains.
