The `description` front-matter calls the Codex pairing "a three-round critical dialogue". The source states three rounds as the *minimum* unit (「AIによるレビューの批判的対話は、次の3ラウンドを最小単位にしています」) and adds that the number of rounds is not fixed — up to three automated cycles per review point, switching to interactive mode when the same finding survives two consecutive cycles. Read alone, the description's phrasing could be taken as a fixed total of three rounds; the body text does not repeat the flattening, and does not carry the cycle cap or the interactive-switch rule at all.
Key Point 2 opens with the unqualified "What to delegate is decided from the direction of information transformation, not from current model performance" — the attribution rests entirely on "on the stated grounds that" later in the same sentence. Every neighbouring key point carries an explicit attribution marker up front ("The stated failure mode", "The author's stated point", "is argued as", "Reported limits"), so this one reads as more general than its siblings even though it is the same kind of claim.
The Context section's comparison to [[DefinedTerm/n-version-programming]] ("the same reasoning ... applied to criticism rather than generation") is the wiki's own framing. The source never mentions N-version programming, nor closed-loop AI review; it argues only that a single model self-reviewing has weak independence of perspective (「観点の独立性は強くありません」). Worth knowing on a re-read that the structural neighbour claim comes from the wiki's linking, not from the post.
