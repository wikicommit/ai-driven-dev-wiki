The phrase "agents that execute human-reviewed implementation plans" is slightly easy to misread. The source says the author writes the plan together with the agent and polishes it over several iterations. The author's review comes afterwards, on the finished result, against the plan's acceptance criteria. Plans are co-authored by the human and the agent, and it is the results that the human reviews.
